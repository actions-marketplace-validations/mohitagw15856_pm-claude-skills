# The brief gallery

Morning-brief recipes people actually run: the sources they read, the selection rules, the template. Post yours, borrow someone else's, and each month the best one is adopted into [pm-duet](../../plugins/pm-duet/) as a named variant with credit to its author. This is how the library grows past what one maintainer can write.

## How to post one

Until Discussions are enabled (TODO, maintainer), post a recipe as a [Show and Tell issue](https://github.com/mohitagw15856/pm-claude-skills/issues/new?template=show-and-tell.yml) titled "Brief recipe: [who it is for]". Include:

1. **Who it is for**: the job and the stakes in one line ("solo founder, misses cost money")
2. **Sources and selection rules**: what it reads and what earns a line, in checkable terms
3. **The template**: the exact section order and line caps
4. **One real morning** (redacted): what it included and, more tellingly, what it correctly left out

## The monthly pick

The maintainer picks one recipe a month on three criteria: the selection rules are checkable without judgement calls, the status words follow the [vocabulary](status-vocabulary.md), and the real-morning sample shows restraint. The pick is added to the bundle with the author's handle in the skill's body and the release notes.
