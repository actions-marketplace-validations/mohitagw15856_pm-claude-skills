🏆 The Skill Cup

Each month, one theme. Write the best new skill for it. Entries are scored the way the library scores itself, with no popularity vote. The winner gets a permanent line on this page and a badge on the skill. Prizes are funded month by month by sponsors.

⚡ The quarterly Skill Hack

Once a quarter, a bigger build: a pack of 2 to 4 skills that work together on one theme, solo or in a team of up to three. Same free checks and the same blind judge as the Cup; the pack scores the mean of its skills. The winning pack ships as a named starter pack.

How to enter

  1. Write skills/<your-skill>/SKILL.md on this month's theme. The expert-to-skill compiler builds one from your answers.
  2. Add at least one eval case for it to evals/cases.json: a real request the skill should handle.
  3. Check it with npx skillspec-check skills/<your-skill>. It must reach L3.
  4. Any language: a skill written in Hindi, Chinese or Polish also competes in its own language track, judged in that language. Latin-script languages add language: es (or their code) to the frontmatter.
  5. Open one pull request with [Cup] in the title. A maintainer runs the scoring and the result is posted on your PR.
